# Gunnery measuring tools

Use **Gunnery tools / bearings & arcs** in the sidebar. Enable the overlay, choose the firing ship and a guide. Stonewall has the supplied sheet's forward 180-degree and port/starboard 120-degree presets. The custom tool has adjustable center and sweep, including quick 90/120/180/240-degree widths. It does not imply that a particular ship carries such a mount.

Measure to the other ship, or Alt-click a local board hex. Shift-click retains placement. Select Overhead gunnery view for a north-up chart. Orange lines show arc limits and center; mint lines mark the sighting ruler and target hex. Radius and 100-yard radial bands are adjustable. Curved bands measure straight distances, while the displayed hex range uses the shortest hex path (rules p. 18). Origins are occupied hex centers, not individual physical gun locations.

The readout gives true bearing, signed bearing from the firing ship's bow, and (when targeting the other ship) the observer's angle from the target bow. Same-hex bearings are undefined. Boundary rays are checked against the entire target hex, not just its center. An intersected hex is flagged for the umpire to apply the greater-portion/exact-half rules on p. 16; no area fractions or legal-shot verdicts are calculated.

The supplied `NEW_IRONCLADS_RULES.pdf`, pp. 15–18, distinguishes numerical arcs from separate broadside/shield pattern cards and sectional gun restrictions. The supplied files do not contain the separate Fields of Fire Pattern Cards. Niagara's large broadside Areas I/II pattern is therefore **not reproduced or certified** by the custom sector. Calibrate against the actual card when available. This tool does not establish loading, blocking ships, terrain, sectional eligibility, rake modifiers, or combat results. Armored pivot guns' discrete port restrictions are not replaced by a continuous custom sector. Turret deck-fitting restrictions are likewise not inferred.

Gunnery settings are temporary visual preferences and are not stored in table saves. They do not move ships or advance scenario time. The older Chart tab's simultaneous Stonewall guides remain available; selecting a gunnery preset disables those older guides to avoid duplicate lines.

Runtime verification covers north-crossing angles, inclusive boundaries, full circles, target-hex ray crossings, hex range, scenario-state isolation, and actual player render captures. Historical accuracy of separate pattern cards is outside those checks.
