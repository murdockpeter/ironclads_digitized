# Stonewall: NavSource reference review

Reviewed 31 October 2026 following the user's reference link:
[NavSource CSS Stonewall photo index](https://www.navsource.net/archives/09/86/86516.htm).

This review recorded the corrections identified after model pass 2 and the evidence used for the subsequent pass.

**Pass-3 follow-up:** the hull planform, supported battery bases, sloping armor, armored forward deck and curved ram have now been revised using this evidence. The third-pass model remains an approximate interpretation; see `Artifacts/Pass3` for its overhead/profile/quarter views and support checks. Detail still awaiting measured plans includes exact hull sections, gun slide/traverse layouts and some deck fittings.

## Corrections supported by the images

1. **Rebuild the hull and batteries as a connected armored structure.** The dry-dock and NH 70813 photographs show the forward battery plating continuing into the bow/hull sides. The pass-2 model's independent elliptical enclosure over a narrowing hull does not reproduce this. Simply scaling down its roof would not correct the underlying hull shape.
2. **Replace the generic pointed hull planform for Stonewall.** The supplied plan shows a much longer region of nearly parallel sides and a fuller stern. Its forward and aft battery bases meet the outer structure, while the pass-2 loft tapers too aggressively beneath them.
3. **Reconsider the battery footprints and slopes.** The plan depicts a comparatively round forward battery and an aft enclosure broader across the beam than fore-and-aft. The present forward enclosure is excessively elongated in plan. Photographs show more pronounced sloping armor integrated into the side plating.
4. **Rebuild the ram from the dry-dock silhouette.** The armor and stem descend into a long projecting ram with a substantial curved transition. The pass-2 closed wedge solves missing geometry but remains a simplified approximation of that profile.
5. **Refine gun mounts and openings.** The plan depicts traversing arrangements and two aft guns directed through angled openings. The current opposed port/starboard display poses and truck/slide fittings are illustrative; accurate reconstruction requires interpreting the plan against the period photographs and testimony.
6. **Add period-visible fittings deliberately.** Bow anchors, hawse openings and chains, armor strakes and seams, mast stays and channels, and stern structure are better supported by these views than by the initial generic fittings.

## Verification for a subsequent modeling pass

- Compare overhead hull/battery silhouettes directly with the reference plan; clearly identify inferred rather than measured dimensions.
- Verify battery base edges have structural support and form intentional armor transitions. Hull closure checks alone do not detect an overhanging separate enclosure.
- Render bow, stern, both broadsides and overhead views with consistent scale and lighting; compare against the period photographs.
- Retain the three-gun Stonewall battery and keep actual gun openings separate from unintended mesh gaps.
- Keep inspection images and geometry statistics alongside the pass-1 and pass-2 baselines.
