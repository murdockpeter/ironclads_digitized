# Newly supplied Stonewall references

Reviewed 31 October 2026:

- `contextonly/ShipSheets/stonewall3.jpg`: photograph of page 114, *The Confederate Steam Navy 1861–1865*. Contains side elevation, plan and four sections. The caption explicitly describes the drawing as **conjectural**, potentially representing Stonewall or Prinz Adalbert. It identifies Wikimedia Commons as the illustration source. The pictured page is therefore not independent confirmation of the similar plan already retained from NavSource.
- `contextonly/ShipSheets/stonewall4.jpg`: photograph of page 113, section on Stonewall. Discusses dimensions and distinguishes waterline length from length between perpendiculars; describes twin propellers and separate rudders. The forward battery is described as stationary, with uncertainty about how many gun ports existed at different stages.

Useful shape checks from the supplied illustration: nearly parallel midbody sides, rounded quarters, strong forward tumblehome, a continuous raised forward hull enclosure, and an extended underwater ram. The front section shows a narrow upper forward cross-section over a broader lower hull. The midship section and aft section can guide an approximate bilge and stern review, but the photographic page is oblique and must not be treated as a dimensionally accurate tracing.

The illustration's side elevation distinguishes the relatively low straight stern battery from the hull-integrated forward gunroom. Its internal circular gunroom outline does not mean a rotating monitor turret. The current bow revision already removes the independent forward drum; next geometry comparisons should examine forecastle roof width, the rear transition into the waist, and the ram's profile against the dry-dock photographs. Exact frame offsets remain unknown.

No source files have been modified or replaced. No additional Stonewall mesh changes are made merely on the basis of this conjectural illustration.
