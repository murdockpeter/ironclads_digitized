# Hampton Roads detail and geography pass — 1.2.2-dev

Build with `Ironclads.Editor.ProjectBuilder.DeliverDetailAndTerrain`, producing `Builds/Windows-HamptonRoads-Detail`. The launcher uses this build; `Start-Ironclads.ps1 -IronFire` selects the continuous miniatures mode. Earlier open players remain separate. No new GitHub release has been published.

## Monitor and Virginia

Monitor's turret has finer circumference/height sampling, four rows of fastenings, detailed hatch dogs and hinges, pilothouse joints and bolts, and smoother XI-inch gun profiles. Virginia has a denser sloping casemate mesh with actual open gunports, finer Brooke/Dahlgren profiles, additional armor fastening rows, roof handholds and funnel seams. Both use four longitudinal rendering sections rather than two. Mesh combining preserves the moving Monitor turret and the shadow settings of cosmetic surface marks.

Armor is divided among subtly different iron tones. Small deterministic shallow dents and scraped/discolored areas blended into the armor material depict illustrative shot bruises. They are cosmetic examples, not an exact historical strike map or a record of hits/damage decided by the umpire. Gun bores, nominal batteries, muzzle attachment points and turret controls retain their existing behavior. Minnesota and the two wreck models retain their previous geometry.

Previous counts: Monitor **64,160 vertices / 32 meshes**, Virginia **65,710 / 28**. The new scene's measured counts are written to `Artifacts/hampton-geometry-counts.csv`; the editor checks that both ships fall between 1.8× and 2.5× their earlier vertex counts. Counts include separate spatial/material mesh sections, and are not draw-call measurements. New ship and close-detail previews are generated by the editor review.

Final measured counts: **Monitor 121,668 vertices / 70 meshes**, **Virginia 136,072 vertices / 77 meshes**. The vertex increases are approximately 1.90× and 2.07×. Mesh counts rise with the finer spatial sections and additional armor materials.

## Reused campaign geography

The source pipeline was found in the neighboring GCBH and Sea of Uncertainty projects:

- GCBH `mission-map/renderer/data/global-land.geojson`: cached **Natural Earth 1:10m land and minor islands**.
- Sea of Uncertainty `tools/build-luzon-elevation.ps1`: **NOAA ETOPO 2022** relief served by ERDDAP.

This project now packages its own Hampton Roads crop in `Assets/Resources/Geography/HamptonRoads.json`, covering 36.86–37.12° N and 76.55–76.18° W. The **95 × 68** elevation grid retains the source's **15-arc-second** sampling, rather than the stride-8 sampling used for the larger Luzon theater. Terrain interpolation supplies gentle local relief at one meter per Unity unit, with no global height exaggeration. Negative/interpolated coastal elevations are blended into a low visible land edge where the generalized coastline and relief grid disagree.

These are **modern, regional datasets**, not an 1862 shoreline, land-cover or harbor-depth survey. The Natural Earth coast omits Old Point Comfort at this scale, so the crop includes a documented approximate footprint for Fort Monroe. Its platform is leveled locally so its moat and walls meet the terrain. Other historical forts, batteries and wharves remain procedural reconstructions. Modern reclamation may remain in the generalized coastline; modern buildings, container terminals and bridges are not added. Grounding, collision, shore fire and line of sight remain manual.

Ground material varies meadow, soil, marsh and woodland colors by world position and elevation. Mixed clusters include conifer, broadleaf and winter/scrub forms with multiple foliage tones and varied heights. This vegetation and coloring are **procedural visual interpretation**, not measured historic land cover. A **Coastal woodland / ETOPO relief** landmark provides a closer inspection view in Scenarios.

Regenerate the crop with Node.js and local build-time dependencies:

```powershell
npm install --prefix .tools earcut polygon-clipping --no-audit --no-fund
node Tools/build-hampton-geography.cjs
```

The script reads the neighboring cached Natural Earth file by default, or accepts another copy as its first argument. It downloads only the small NOAA elevation subset. Polygon clipping and Earcut triangulation handle disconnected coastal polygons before Unity builds the terrain; no JavaScript dependency is required by the player.

Sources: [Natural Earth land](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/10m-land/), [NOAA ETOPO ERDDAP metadata](https://coastwatch.pfeg.noaa.gov/erddap/info/ETOPO_2022_v1_15s/index.html), ETOPO DOI **10.25921/fd45-gt74**. Natural Earth is public domain; NOAA attribution and terms are retained in the packaged metadata. This crop is not for navigation.

Verification: editor checks ship counts, batteries, turret/muzzle behavior, imported elevation, land/water placement at the duel and Fort Monroe, clustered foliage, surface normals, support ships and scenario switching. Standalone player checks exercise salvos, continuous movement and persistence; TCP checks use two real local processes. Ferrol regression remains available in the same build.
